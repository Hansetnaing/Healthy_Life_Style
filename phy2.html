It should be the right amount

In order for the body to grow and develop in a healthy manner, we must not forget to include the necessary nutrients in proportion when preparing food. 50 percent of rice 25% meat 15 percent of vegetables; Beans 10 percent and so on. So that the body needs to grow healthy and develop protein, starch fiber It will be full of vitamins and minerals. It is also the best support for strengthening the immune system.

Be clean and fresh

The fresher the food, the higher the nutritional value. So, don't forget to prepare clean and fresh food in order to get full nutrition. Doing this not only provides full nutrition but also prevents unwanted diseases. So, when choosing foods, take care of cleanliness

Eat fruits and vegetables.

Fruits are the best source of natural fiber. So eat fruits regularly. Eating fruit rather than drinking juice provides more fiber.
Eat colorful vegetables in your daily diet. Eating this will fill you with the necessary vitamins.

A variety of fish

Eating a variety of fish improves eye health. Especially salmon. These fish contain healthy omega-3 fatty acids. Omega-3 fatty acids improve vision and support retinal health. It can also prevent dry eyes.

A variety of eggs

All kinds of eggs have vitamin A, layman zeaxanthin, It is rich in zinc, etc., which helps in eye health. Vitamin A is especially important for the cornea. Lutein and zeaxanthin can cause blurring of vision that occurs with age. Reduces the risk of cataracts. Zinc helps with night vision.

Dairy products

Milk and yogurt are good for eye health. Dairy products contain vitamin A and zinc. Vitamin A can protect the uterus by reducing damage, and zinc helps the liver deliver vitamin A to the eyes. In addition, zinc improves night vision and prevents cataracts.
