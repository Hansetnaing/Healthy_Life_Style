What is
Physical Health?

Physical health refers to the overall condition of the body and its ability to perform daily activities effectively and without undue fatigue or strain. It encompasses various aspects such as cardiovascular health, muscular strength, flexibility, and body composition. Maintaining physical health involves regular exercise, proper nutrition, adequate rest, and avoidance of harmful substances like tobacco and excessive alcohol. Good physical health not only enhances physical abilities but also promotes mental well-being, resilience against illnesses, and longevity.
