Mental wellbeing

Give your mind support and space.

Mental wellbeing affects the way we think, feel, connect, and cope. It changes over time, and it deserves care just as much as physical health.

When stress builds

Notice, pause, and respond with care.

Stress can affect sleep, appetite, focus, mood, and the body. There is no single solution, but small supportive actions can make a real difference.

  • Name what you are feeling without judging it.
  • Take short breaks to breathe, stretch, or step outside.
  • Keep contact with trusted friends, family, or community.
  • Talk with a qualified mental-health professional if stress feels difficult to manage.

Everyday supports

Gentle practices for difficult days

Rest your attention

Try a few minutes away from notifications, with a quiet activity such as reading, walking, or listening to music.

Keep a routine

Regular meals, sleep, and movement can provide useful structure when life feels uncertain.

Ask for help

Speaking to someone you trust is a strength. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line now.
